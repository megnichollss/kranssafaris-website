Pricing and Logistics

What's Covered

Prices Include:

  • Pick up and drop off (Agreed location)
  • Hunting license fees
  • Professional hunter, 4x4 vehicle & tracker
  • Support crew, camp staff, skinners and chefs
  • Transport while on safari
  • All meals, beverages, WiFi, and laundry

What's Not Included:

  • International and domestic flights
  • Shipping & taxidermy
  • Trophy handling fees
  • Rifle hire & ammunition per safari
  • Gratuities for staff & professional hunter
  • Meals & liquor outside of camps & hunting areas

Rates & Policy

Rates

All prices and quotes are in USD and will change per season. Contact us for a personalized quote.

Bookings & Deposits

Once you contact us, bookings take place directly with Krans Safaris. A minimum of 40% of the total daily rate is needed to confirm your booking.

Full payment is due within a week of arrival. Trophy fees will be settled in full at the end of the hunt.

Insurance

We strongly recommend all clients obtain comprehensive travel insurance.

Cancellation Policy

Less than 6 months prior: Deposit forfeited if dates can't be rescheduled within the same season.

More than 6 months prior: 50% of the booking is non-refundable but can be applied to a safari booking within the season.
