Coastal Excursions & Logistics

Morgan Bay Experience

Location and Scenery

Morgan Bay is a celebrated holiday destination, set on the picturesque Southern end of the Wild Coast. It is known for its stunning mile-long beach, pristine estuary, and dramatic sea cliffs.

The coastal retreat is conveniently situated just 1 hour and 15 minutes from our main hunting concession, allowing for a diverse safari experience.

Coastal Activities

Beyond the water, Morgan Bay offers a wealth of leisure activities for the entire family:

  • Guided Bird Watching
  • Challenging Mountain Biking Trails
  • Insightful Township Tours
  • Surfing and Swimming
  • Scenic Horse Riding along the beach

Rates & Policy

Rates

All prices and quotes are in USD and change per season, based on whether you choose a day trip or a multi-day package. Contact us for a personalized quote.

Bookings are handled directly with Krans Safaris. A minimum of 40% of the excursion rate is needed to confirm your booking.

Cancellation Policy

More than 6 months prior: 50% of the booking is non-refundable but can be applied to your next excursion booking within the season.
