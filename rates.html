Inclusions & Exclusions

Included:

  • Pick up and drop off (Agreed location)
  • Hunting license fees
  • Professional hunter (vehicle & tracker)
  • Support crew, camp staff, skinners and chefs
  • Transport while on safari
  • All meals, beverages, WiFi, and laundry

Not Included:

  • International and domestic flights
  • Shipping & taxidermy
  • Trophy handling fees (per safari)
  • Rifle hire & ammunition per safari
  • Gratuities for staff & professional hunter
  • Meals & liquor outside of camps & hunting areas

Booking & Cancellation Policies

Rates & Bookings

All prices and quotes are in USD and are subject to change per season. Once you contact us with your enquiry, bookings will take place directly with Krans Safaris.

Deposits & Payment

A minimum of 40% of the total daily rate is needed to confirm your booking. Full payment is due within a week of arrival. Trophy fees will be settled in full at the end of the hunt.

Insurance & Policy

We highly recommend that all clients obtain comprehensive travel insurance.

  • Less than 6 months prior: Deposit forfeited if dates can't be rescheduled within the same season.
  • More than 6 months prior: 50% of the booking is non-refundable but can be applied to a future safari within the season.
