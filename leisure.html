Scenic & Leisure Activities

Beyond the Hunt: Explore the Eastern Cape

Krans Safaris offers seamless access to the Eastern Cape's most breathtaking attractions. We handle all the logistics, from dedicated road transfers to arranging specialized accommodation and private bookings, ensuring your entire trip is effortless.

Curated Excursions

Scenic Flights up the Wild Coast

Experience the raw beauty of the Eastern Cape coastline from a unique perspective. Our scenic flights offer unparalleled views of the Wild Coast, including rugged cliffs, untouched beaches, and sweeping estuaries. A truly unforgettable aerial adventure. (We will send you further information on flight options and routes upon booking.)

Golf Day at Olivewood

Spend a relaxing day at the esteemed Olivewood Private Estate & Golf Club. Enjoy a round on this beautifully maintained course, which seamlessly blends luxury golfing with the natural Eastern Cape landscape. All green fees and transport are arranged for your convenience.

Wild Coast Leisure at Morgan Bay

For a relaxed coastal interlude, we organize easy access to activities around Morgan Bay. These include bird watching, mountain biking, local township tours, surfing, and scenic horse riding. This picturesque area perfectly complements the intensity of the safari experience.

Accommodation & Stayovers

Addo Elephant Park Excursion

Experience one of South Africa’s premier wildlife sanctuaries. We highly recommend a dedicated **2-night stayover at Addo** to fully immerse yourself in the park and maximize game viewing opportunities, specializing in close encounters with elephants and the Big 5.

Pre-Safari Stay at Crawfords Beach Lodge

Begin or end your safari in luxury with a **2-night sleep over at Crawfords Beach Lodge**. Located right on the beach, it provides an exquisite setting for relaxing before or after your adventure. We include transfers to and from this premium location.

The Morgan Bay Hotel Base

The Morgan Bay Hotel is the perfect base for coastal activities, including offshore fishing and scenic flights. We utilize this location for short, convenient stays, offering comfortable accommodations with stunning coastal views to facilitate your excursions.
